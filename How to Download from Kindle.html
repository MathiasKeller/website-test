Download Instructions

Follow these steps to find and transfer your content.

  1. Open the archive library in a new tab.

    Anna's Archive

  2. Search for what you are looking for in the search box.

  3. Click on the content that you want. Make sure the format is EPUB, AWZ, or AWZ3.

  4. Scroll down to the slow downloads section.

  5. Download a file from the slow downloads link.

  6. If the format is EPUB, you will need to convert it to AWZ3 for compatibility.

  1. Go to the online conversion tool.

    Convert EPUB

  2. Upload your downloaded EPUB file to the converter.

  3. Click convert and wait for the process to finish, then download the new AWZ3 formatted file.

  4. Connect your Kindle to your computer and upload the file to your Kindle. You can drop it directly into the top-level folder on your Kindle—you don't need to put it in any specific folders.
